📌 Project Overview

The NEXORA Supply Industries Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze business performance across multiple dimensions.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and yearly business trends. It transforms raw business data into meaningful visualizations, helping stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify patterns, and make data-driven decisions.

The primary objective of this project is to simplify complex business data and present actionable insights through an intuitive and interactive dashboard.

🎯 Business Objectives
Monitor overall revenue and profitability.
Analyze monthly profit trends and identify performance fluctuations.
Identify products generating the highest and lowest profits.
Understand new customer acquisition and returning customer behavior.
Evaluate yearly revenue and profit performance.
Track important business KPIs through an interactive dashboard.

🔍 Dashboard Features
1. Monthly Profit Analysis
Tracks monthly profit performance.
Identifies fluctuations in profitability.
Highlights changes in business performance throughout the year.

2. Product Profitability Analysis
Compares profit generated by different products.
Identifies the most profitable products.
Helps understand product-level financial performance.

3. New vs. Returning Customers
Compares monthly customer acquisition and returning customers.
Identifies customer behavior patterns.
Helps evaluate customer retention performance.
4. Yearly Revenue & Profit Analysis
Compares annual revenue and profit.
Tracks financial performance across different years.
Helps identify changes in business growth and profitability.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

DAX: Calculated measures and KPI development.
Data Modeling: Organizing data for analytical reporting.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard provides several useful business observations:

Returning customers account for 59.16% of the customer base.
Perfect Fitness Perfect Rip Deck is the highest-profit product among the products displayed.
